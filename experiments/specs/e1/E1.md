# `experiments/specs/e1/`

Paper E1; public preset `paper/e1`.

Plugin package for experiment `e1`.

`e1` is the baseline mirror-room cube task: the mirror is present, the scene
uses the standard two-room layout with 30° turn actions (`a`/`d`), and the task
is the shared `cube_task`. Cube and agent colors are sampled from the shared
ten-color pool each run. The mirror opening is widened to 8.0 world units, and
the `e1` cube slots are restricted to positions that lie inside that opening so
their reflections are physically visible through the mirror rather than being
faked outside the aperture.
