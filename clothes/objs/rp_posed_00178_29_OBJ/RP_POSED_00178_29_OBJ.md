# `assets/mujoco/objs/rp_posed_00178_29_OBJ/`

External avatar asset pack used by experiment `e8`.

## Contents

- `rp_posed_00178_29.obj`: main mesh imported by `e8`.
- `tex/`: texture maps associated with the avatar.
- optional preview/reference images.

## Notes

- `e8` scans OBJ assets recursively under `assets/mujoco/objs/`.
- The loader selects a color texture automatically (prefers `basecolor/albedo/diffuse` names).
