# `conf/experiment/extras/`

Shared presets preserve direct perception (e0), exploration (e6), clothed
embodiment (e8), and the original mirror/long corridor task (`corridor`).

Use `experiment=extras/<name>`. Legacy numeric presets remain available.
Outputs are written beneath `outputs/extras`; no existing data are moved.
Clothed avatars are needed only for experiments that explicitly use them.

Local front-identity and mirror-side-choice presets may also be present in a
research checkout. Their source and integration are outside the paper-alignment
commit and need separate review before publication.
